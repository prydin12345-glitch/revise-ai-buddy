## Verified requirements and specification boundaries

The course ID is `pearson_edexcel_alevel_biology_b_9bi0`; the paper ID is `9bi0_01`; component `9BI0/01`; contract v1; specification identity `issue_4_november_2018`; assessment tier `not_tiered`. Qualification weighting is 30%. There is no Foundation/Higher control.

| Included topic | Permitted planning content |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Biological Molecules | Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, DNA/replication/gene expression, enzyme mechanisms and investigations, ions and water. |
| 2 Cells, Viruses and Reproduction of Living Things | Ultrastructure/microscopy, viruses, mitosis/meiosis, assortment/crossing over, animal and plant reproduction. |
| 3 Classification and Biodiversity | Classification/molecular evidence, natural selection/speciation, biodiversity and conservation. The specified supplied diversity expression is `D = N(N−1)/Σn(n−1)`, not OCR's alternative expression. |
| 4 Exchange and Transport | Surface-area relationships, membranes/water potential, gas exchange, circulation/intrinsic cardiac conduction, oxygen affinity, tissue fluid and plant transport. |
| 5 Energy for Biological Processes | Respiration, ATP/chemiosmosis, photosynthetic spectra/chromatography, chloroplasts, Calvin cycle and limiting factors. |
| 6 Microbiology and Pathogens | Aseptic culture/growth, pathogens, antibiotics/resistance, malaria-control evidence, immunity and vaccination. |
| 7 Modern Genetics | Genomes, PCR/sequencing/profiling, gene regulation/epigenetics, stem cells and recombinant technology. |

Topics 8–10 are outside Paper 1. The deterministic scope gate checks task/context, private keys and raw resources for known later-paper requirements: inheritance crosses/Punnett squares/pedigrees/linkage/Hardy–Weinberg/chi-squared inheritance/drift; nervous/endocrine/homeostatic control; and ecosystem energy flow/cycles/succession/distribution sampling. A correct topic tag cannot conceal these. This is a known-boundary validator, not an exhaustive scientific classifier. Valid Topic 2 meiosis, Topic 3 natural selection/conservation and Topic 4 SAN/AVN conduction are deliberately retained. Saved out-of-scope profile topics fail preflight before a model call.

The mathematics minimum is **10% Level 2 or above across the three qualification papers**, not an exact Paper 1 percentage. Quantitative contexts include rates, units/magnification, chromatographic ratios, growth/logarithms and supplied experimental measurements. Core practicals 1–13 correspond to included topics; 14 (gibberellin), 15/16 (ecological sampling/distribution) are outside this component. Written practice does not award Pearson's separately assessed practical endorsement.

Appendix 7 command meanings inform tasks: Calculate shows quantitative working; Describe reports relevant features; Explain links biological reasons/mechanisms; Assess weighs relevant factors; Evaluate reaches a supported judgement; Compare and contrast includes similarities/differences; Deduce/Determine use supplied evidence; Plot draws from data; Devise plans a procedure; Criticise examines a proposal/data critically. Choice questions use explicit selection wording. Pearson-only imperative recognition is scoped to this course; narrative uses of “assess” or “devise” do not become student tasks.
