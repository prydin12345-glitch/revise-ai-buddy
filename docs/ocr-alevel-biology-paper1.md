# OCR A-level Biology A — H420/01

## Release scope

Adds explicit course/paper selection for **Biology A H420, Paper 1: Biological processes**. Full A-level is untiered: neither Foundation nor Higher applies. A custom subject name such as `Biology Higher` may identify the subject, but never chooses a tier, OCR course or paper implicitly.

H420/02, H420/03 and Biology B (Advancing Biology) H422 remain unavailable. AS H020 is not enabled by this release. H420/04 is the separate practical endorsement, not a generated paper.

H420/01 assesses Modules **1, 2, 3 and 5**, with **100 marks in 135 minutes**, all questions compulsory. Section A is fifteen one-mark MCQs, followed by 85 written marks beginning at Q16. H420/02 also has a 15-mark MCQ section; H420/03 does not. Those later components need their own implementation.

## MCQ and marking behaviour

- Plan locks Q1–15 ahead of Q16(a), including after truncated output, completion and repair. The old general sort could put a parent-with-parts before a bare question number; guided generation now follows its saved plan order.
- Exactly four distinct descriptive options are stored in A–D order. Existing option aliases and group repair parsing remain in use. No option shuffle is introduced.
- One private key must identify exactly one stored choice. Ambiguous/malformed keys block finalisation or marking; they do not become a zero score.
- OCR MCQs are marked deterministically from a selected A–D letter or the exact saved option text. A wrong, blank or multiple selection earns zero. MCQ-only marking requires no provider call. Ownership, release and existing quota controls remain in force.
- Written answers retain model marking. Planned six-mark responses require OCR three-level schemes; scientific content determines the level and communication determines the mark within it.
- OCR Paper 1 scope deliberately allows Module 5 neural/endocrine mechanisms, ADH/nephron, Calvin and Krebs cycles. AQA Paper 1 and GCSE exclusions must not be inherited.
- Canonical tables/graphs are required where planned. PDF export preserves saved A–D order and stops if an inline option list conflicts. OCR MCQ pagination reserves room for the answer box.
