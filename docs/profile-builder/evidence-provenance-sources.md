## What the sources establish

The current specification's physical page 9 contains an **A-level / Assessments / Paper 1** block after the AS material. Its explicit assessment facts are Sections 1–5 and 6.1 (Periodic motion), 2 hours, 85 marks, and a mixture of short/long answer questions and 25 multiple-choice questions. These facts must remain attached to that A-level block; the preceding AS Paper 2 block has different time/marks.

Both reviewed Paper 1 covers explicitly state the following short instruction fragments on physical page 1:

- Selection: **“Answer all questions.”**
- Calculator use: **“You are expected to use a scientific calculator where appropriate.”**
- Total: **“The maximum mark for this paper is 85.”**
- Timing: **“Time allowed: 2 hours”**.

These are direct assessment instructions, rather than a calculator mention in the specification's mathematical-skills discussion. A narrowly scoped reviewed Paper 1 supplement can cite those cover instructions for calculator permission and compulsory selection when the uploaded specification omits them. It must retain the actual cover URL/checksum/page, authority and review provenance. The two series corroborate these particular rules; they do not prove an unchanging layout for every future sitting.

The physical page 10 **Paper 3** block explicitly distinguishes a compulsory Section A and one chosen option in Section B. Therefore absence of choice wording is not a safe general rule, and this Paper 1 supplement must not apply to Paper 3 or its section codes.
