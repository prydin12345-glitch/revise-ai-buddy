## Facts and boundaries registered

The eight entries cover **separate GCSE Physics 8463 and Chemistry 8462**, Papers 1 and 2, Foundation and Higher. They do not cover Combined Science: Trilogy/Synergy or another board/qualification. Each full assessment is **100 marks, 105 minutes and 50% of its GCSE**, as stated on specification physical pages 9 and 10. The supported response categories derive from “Multiple choice, structured, closed short answer and open response”; their contribution is not fixed by this registration. The exact component/tier, scientific-calculator requirement and answer-all instruction are cross-checked against each June 2025 cover (physical page 1).

| Physics Paper 2 | Forces; Waves; Magnetism and electromagnetism; Space physics | Energy changes/transfers through heating and mechanical/electrical work, and energy conservation; not all Paper 1 topics |
| Chemistry Paper 1 | Atomic structure and the periodic table; Bonding, structure, and the properties of matter; Quantitative chemistry; Chemical changes; Energy changes | None additionally inferred |
| Chemistry Paper 2 | The rate and extent of chemical change; Organic chemistry; Chemical analysis; Chemistry of the atmosphere; Using resources | Fundamental concepts/principles from sections 4.1–4.3; not standalone Chemical changes or Energy changes |

- Physical page **17** in both specifications explains `(HT only)`: Higher-only content must be excluded at Foundation. Separate-science-only content is valid in these entries; it must not be silently reused as Combined Science.
- Mathematical requirements: Physics physical page **85**, Chemistry **99**. The evidence requires mathematics at the appropriate assessment demand. It does not invent a fixed percentage allocation for either individual paper.
- Practical assessment: Physics physical page **87**, Chemistry **101**. The source minimum of **15% of overall qualification marks** is not represented as exactly 15% in every paper.
- Physics covers require the enclosed Physics Equations Sheet. This registration neither copies that sheet nor supplies an arbitrary image. Generated calculation tasks must include any formula information needed to answer them. Chemistry covers require the enclosed periodic table; generated questions needing atomic/element data must supply that information within supported resources rather than refer to a missing sheet.
- Foundation/Higher are explicit user choices; an unknown tier cannot select an entry. The resolver binds country, educational stage, qualification, board, course, component, version and tier. Saved version identifiers are not silently upgraded.

## Deliberate Examly choices and limitations

No AI output has been produced or externally quality-reviewed during this work. The generic builder still uses its shared supported compulsory response/table/graph adapters. Parent/group counts, question mark distribution and short-practice length are Examly development choices. Reviewed diagrams/passages, optional routes and other unsupported mandatory formats require their existing explicit adapters; retrieving an official PDF does not enable them or award a gold star.

The source records keep concise factual excerpts, physical page locations and exact short references. They do not reproduce the full specification content or claim a complete per-subtopic scientific validator for these new courses. Their purpose is to remove avoidable code entry/upload work while preserving provenance and fail-closed structural validation.

Source URL resolution uses an exact reviewed entry. A specification URL shared by several papers/tiers is ambiguous without that entry; it never picks the first match. Runtime open-web fetching remains disabled. The read-only maintenance downloader permits Pearson's established host and AQA's exact public asset-project PDF paths on `cdn.sanity.io`; it retains DNS/address/redirect/TLS/type/size checks and sends no forwarded credentials.
