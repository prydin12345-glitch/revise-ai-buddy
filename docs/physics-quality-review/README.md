## Scope and limits

The official [AQA specification/source record](../profile-builder/assessment-choice-sources.md) identifies A-level Physics 7408 Paper 2: sections 6.2, 7 and 8, with assumed earlier knowledge. Its 85 marks, 120 minutes and 60 written plus 25 MCQ marks are source facts. Six ten-mark written parent groups are an Examly development layout, not an official fixed parent count.

No arbitrary official topic-mark quotas or requirement to test every syllabus item is invented. Topic balance and demand depend on the saved blueprint. The private paper's nuclear question tagged as assumed knowledge and its weak gamma-versus-background evidence cannot be re-audited without that paper's full data. Generation instructions now require diverse assessment tasks, suitable reasoning, explicit electrical-measurement assumptions and discriminating radiation evidence, but those prompts are not a general scientific verifier. Review the rebuilt profile's topic/format/demand plan and the fresh output externally.

An existing owner profile with conflicting edition metadata must be rebuilt or corrected from the matching source before new generation. This patch does not silently relabel its PDF, change historical questions/grades or erase attempted exams. The current [official Physics specification](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p28bar15/green/85944e7114a6a88e8a769b108949133ce30d3cba.pdf) is Version 1.4 July 2026; use a matching source/profile rather than assuming the user's older upload was that version. Old edition support needs matching evidence rather than a claimed current issue.

They are not a signed-in live-paper audit, physical iPad/iPhone certification, real Gemini output review or a 10/10 rating.

[Post-merge Lovable handoff](deployment.md) lists only the affected functions. No migration, generated database-type edit, deployment, merge, publication, paid model call, live login or live-data edit is performed here. The existing owner-only testing-limit exemption is preserved unchanged; this PR does not apply or broaden it.

After review, green CI and the exact deployments, begin with one fresh short Paper 2 and an external quality review. Generate a fresh full Paper 2 only after that passes, then review it externally too. A quality-gate failure is bounded and visible; it is not a promise that a provider call was free or that a paid reservation is refunded.
